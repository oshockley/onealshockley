# Profile Cover Art Setup

## Quick Setup Instructions

1. **Save your futuristic cover art image** to this directory as:
   ```
   profile-cover.jpg
   ```

2. The image should be:
   - **Recommended dimensions**: 1200x900px (4:3 aspect ratio)
   - **Format**: JPG or PNG
   - **File size**: Under 500KB for optimal loading

3. Your image will be automatically displayed in the hero section with:
   - 3D depth effects and hover animations
   - Subtle cyan glow that enhances the futuristic aesthetic
   - Screen blend mode to integrate with the black/white theme
   - Responsive design for mobile devices

## Alternative Image Names

If you prefer a different filename, update line 139 in `index.html`:
```html
<img src="profile-cover.jpg" alt="O'Neal Shockley - Full Stack Developer" class="cover-image">
```

Change `profile-cover.jpg` to your preferred filename.

## Design Notes

The cover art is styled to:
- Blend seamlessly with the monochrome black/grey/white theme
- Preserve the cyan/teal holographic elements from your image
- Add subtle white glows around the edges for depth
- Include a floating "Available for Hire" badge
- Show your key stats (100% Satisfaction, 39+ Projects)

Enjoy your futuristic developer profile showcase! 🚀
